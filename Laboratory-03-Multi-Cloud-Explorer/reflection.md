# Mission Reflection



AWS was the cloud platform that interested me the most. I like that it has many services that can be used for different types of projects. It can provide servers, storage, databases, and other tools for businesses. I also learned that AWS can support a company as it grows.



AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similar services. All three can provide virtual machines, storage, networking, databases, and security features. They also have online consoles where users can manage their cloud resources. Even though the services have different names, many of them have similar purposes.



The needs of a business are important when selecting a cloud platform. A company should look at its budget, type of work, security needs, number of users, and future plans. For example, a university that uses Microsoft products may find Azure useful. A company working on AI may choose Google Cloud because of its AI services.



I learned that a cloud provider should be chosen based on the client's needs. I should not choose a platform just because it is popular. I need to understand the problem first and then find the services that can help solve it. This activity also helped me improve my skills in comparing cloud platforms.
